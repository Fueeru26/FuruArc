# Code of Conduct

FuruArc is a small project made by people who like games, translation and tinkering. This page says how we want everyone to treat each other in issues, pull requests and discussions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. Many contributors are not native English speakers, and many are learning.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Assume good faith, and say so when something is unclear instead of guessing.
- Credit the work of others, including the format documentation and tools FuruArc builds on.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting copyrighted game files, keys, cracks or anything that is only useful for piracy. Share a file list or a hex dump instead (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Spam, advertising and off-topic promotion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@Fueeru26](https://github.com/Fueeru26) privately through GitHub (for example through the contact links in the app under About). Reports are handled in confidence.

This policy applies to all project spaces on GitHub.
